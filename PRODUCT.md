# MovieStream Pro

## Product Purpose
A cinematic streaming aggregator that lets users discover, browse, and watch movies and TV shows through a premium, immersive interface. The platform aggregates multiple third-party streaming sources behind a unified player experience.

## Users
- **Primary**: Entertainment enthusiasts (18-35) who want a visually premium streaming experience on both desktop and mobile.
- **Mobile-heavy**: Majority access from mid-range Android devices (MediaTek G99 chipset class) with constrained GPU/CPU budgets.
- **Context**: Evening leisure browsing on phones in dimly lit rooms, or desktop viewing on personal monitors.

## Register
brand

## Brand & Tone
- **Visual identity**: Dark, cinematic, premium. Inspired by theatrical experiences.
- **Color**: Sage green accent (#84a98c / #98c1a9) on deep obsidian black (#000000). Restrained palette with organic sage for interactive elements and ratings.
- **Typography**: Bold, uppercase, tracking-heavy headings. Clean body text.
- **Tone**: Confident, minimal, no unnecessary UI chrome. Content (posters, backdrops) is the star.

## Anti-references
- Generic streaming clones with flat white cards
- Overly playful or rounded UI (not Spotify-like)
- SaaS dashboard aesthetics
- Neon-on-black crypto aesthetics

## Strategic Principles
1. **Content first**: The movie/show imagery should dominate. UI elements recede.
2. **Performance is the experience**: Laggy = broken. Smooth 60fps on mid-range mobile is non-negotiable.
3. **Progressive disclosure**: Show what matters (player, poster, title), reveal the rest on scroll.
4. **Cinematic atmosphere**: Gradients, vignettes, and subtle overlays create depth without expensive GPU operations.
